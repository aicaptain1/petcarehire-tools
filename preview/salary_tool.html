Pet Care Salary Calculator (2026)

Look up official pay for veterinary technicians, veterinary assistants, veterinarians, animal caretakers and receptionists. Pick a job and a state — and a metro area if you want — to see the median, the typical range, and how it compares with the U.S. figure. Then jump to open listings on Pet Care Hire.

Every number comes from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics, May 2025, the latest official release. We do not invent or fill in suppressed values. If BLS did not publish a figure, you will see n/a. If a state has no current openings in the job you picked, the button says so and takes you to that job nationwide instead of an empty list.

How to use this calculator

  1. Choose a job. Veterinary technician is the BLS occupation that includes certified, registered and licensed vet techs (CVT, RVT, LVT). Receptionist pay is for receptionists in all industries, not clinics only, and is labeled that way.
  2. Choose a state, or leave it on the United States to see the national figures. The District of Columbia and Puerto Rico are included.
  3. Optionally choose a metro area, such as Los Angeles–Long Beach–Anaheim. Metros that sit in more than one state appear in each of those states.
  4. Toggle between annual and hourly pay. The range bar shows the 10th to 90th percentile, the middle 50%, the median, and the U.S. median for comparison.
  5. Use See open jobs to open the matching category and state filter on Pet Care Hire, or subscribe to the free weekly newsletter.

Frequently asked questions

How much do vet techs make in the United States?

Veterinary technologists and technicians had a U.S. median wage of $47,380 a year, or $22.78 an hour, in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. The average (mean) was $49,120 a year. Use the calculator to see pay in a specific state or metro area.

Where does this data come from?

Every figure in the calculator comes from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS) program, May 2025 estimates, released in 2026. Nothing is estimated or filled in by Pet Care Hire. If BLS did not publish a number for an area, the calculator shows n/a.

Why is there no salary for my city?

BLS publishes wages for states, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico and metropolitan statistical areas, not for every town. If your city is part of a metro area, choose that metro. If BLS suppressed the estimate because the sample was too small, the tool shows n/a rather than a guess.

Does this include certified, registered or licensed vet techs?

Yes, in the veterinary technician numbers. BLS does not publish a separate wage for CVT, RVT or LVT credentials. Those titles are counted in occupation 29-2056, Veterinary Technologists and Technicians. See our certified and registered vet tech salary guide for more on how the titles work.

Why is the receptionist salary labeled “all industries”?

The official BLS occupation is Receptionists and Information Clerks (43-4171), which covers reception work across the economy, not veterinary clinics alone. Clinic pay can be different. The jobs button on this page still takes you to veterinary receptionist and client-service listings on Pet Care Hire.

Are the animal caretaker wages for groomers only?

No. BLS groups groomers, kennel staff, dog walkers, pet sitters and similar roles as animal caretakers (39-2021). Experienced groomers who build a client base can earn more than the median. The jobs button opens grooming, kennel, walking and daycare listings.

How often is this updated?

BLS typically releases a new OEWS vintage once a year, in the spring, for the previous May. This page uses May 2025, the latest official release as of November 2026. The figures are not adjusted for inflation after the reference month.

Do these numbers include benefits or self-employed pay?

No. OEWS figures are wages for people on payrolls. They exclude the self-employed and do not include benefits. Percentiles describe the wage distribution, not years of experience.
